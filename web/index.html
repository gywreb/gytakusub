Add a manga page or screenshot

Take a screenshot in any app or reader, then paste it here — or pick it from Photos. Your images go only to the Claude API with your own key.

Tip: on iPhone, tap the screenshot preview → Share → Copy, then come back and tap Paste.

Chapter links need your own fetcher. A web page may not read other sites — the browser forbids it — so a small free Cloudflare Worker on your account opens the chapter and passes its pages to this app. Set it up once (about 10 minutes): step-by-step guide, then enter its address and key in Settings → Chapter fetcher.

No fetcher? Screenshot mode works with any site.
