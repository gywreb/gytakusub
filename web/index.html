Set up chapter links

To open a chapter from another site, Gytakusub needs a small fetcher on your own free Cloudflare account. A web page isn't allowed to read other sites (the browser blocks it), but your fetcher can: it opens the chapter and passes its pages to this app. It stores nothing and never sees your Claude key.

About 5 minutes, once. You need a free Cloudflare account and a GitHub or GitLab account. A computer is easiest; afterwards, a link connects your phone. Screenshot mode works without any of this.

Already have a fetcher address and key (for example, someone shared theirs)? Go straight to step 3.

  1. Create your access key

    Only someone with this key can use your fetcher. Keep it private.

    —

  2. Deploy the fetcher to your Cloudflare account

    Deploy to Cloudflare ↗

    1. Sign in to Cloudflare, or create a free account and confirm your email.
    2. Connect GitHub or GitLab when asked. Cloudflare copies the fetcher into a new repository on your account and deploys it from there. You don't pick an existing repository.
    3. On the setup page:
      • Git account: your own GitHub or GitLab account.
      • Repository name: a name your account doesn't use yet, for example gytakusub-fetcher. If you already have a repository called gytakusub, don't reuse that name. A private repository is fine: the key is stored in Cloudflare, not in the repository.
      • Worker name: keep the suggestion or use gytakusub-fetcher. It becomes part of the address.
      • Where GYT_KEY is listed, replace CHANGE_ME with your key from step 1.
    4. Deploy, and wait until the build finishes (about a minute).
    5. Open the Worker's page (Cloudflare dashboard → Workers & Pages → your Worker) and copy its address, which ends in .workers.dev.
    Forgot the key, or deployed without it?

    In Cloudflare, open the Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets. Add (or edit) a Secret named GYT_KEY with your key, then Deploy.

    No GitHub or GitLab account? Use the command line on a computer with Node.js

    Download the repository (zip), open a terminal in its fetcher folder, and run:

    npx wrangler login
npx wrangler secret put GYT_KEY
npx wrangler deploy

    Paste your key when asked; deploy prints the address.

    Cloudflare's own help, if a screen looks different: Deploy to Cloudflare · secrets. The steps were checked against those pages on .

  3. Connect

Update your fetcher

When Gytakusub says your fetcher is out of date: in the repository Cloudflare created on your GitHub or GitLab, open worker.js and edit it. Replace its whole content with this app's version (), and commit. Cloudflare deploys the change by itself; then Connect again.
